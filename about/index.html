About me

Hi, I'm Sławek (pronounced "SWAH-vek") — a Java developer with almost 20 years of commercial experience. I like bash, scripts, Android and anything that's a problem to solve.

What this site is

A personal wiki: notes, links and solutions I don't want to look up twice. Articles I found worth remembering, command lines that saved me, and the occasional post. If something here helps you too, that's a bonus.

What I do

  • Write software in Java — almost 20 years of it commercially, and I still keep an eye on what each new JDK brings.
  • Automate and glue things together with bash and scripts.
  • Tinker with Android until it behaves the way I want.
  • Solve problems — debugging, fixing, and whatever the problem requires learning next.
  • Collect good reading on programming, health and economics.
  • Cook, and write down the recipes that worked.

Off the keyboard

Outside of work I'm a happy father of three girls and husband of one ;). I try to keep moving in my free time — running or on a bike.

Tech stack

This site is plain HTML with a handful of hand-written Web Components, hosted on GitHub Pages — no framework, no build step. Same idea as the notes: keep it simple enough to still understand it in a few years.
